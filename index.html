Why Early Detection Matters

National property and hospitality loss data shows why monitoring mechanical rooms, water lines, and walk-in refrigeration catches costly failures before they escalate.

70.2%

Water leads multifamily claims.

Water damage accounts for 70.2% of multifamily property insurance claims, with reported commercial water claim averages of $11,098 to $15,000+ across industry studies.

>60% RH

Humidity, condensate & pumps.

Indoor humidity above 60% drives condensation and moisture damage, while clogged condensate pans and failing sump or lift pumps trigger repeat weekend callbacks.

$20K-$40K

Walk-in cooler & freezer loss.

A single overnight compressor failure or propped walk-in door can cost hotel kitchens and foodservice operators $20,000-$40,000 in spoiled inventory and lost revenue.

Silent Flow

Running toilets & hidden leaks.

Point sensors catch drips on the floor, while optional continuous-flow monitoring flags continuous or abnormal water use (such as running toilets) before a surprise utility bill arrives.

FAQ

Common questions.

Straight answers to the operational questions property managers and hotel operators ask before starting a pilot.

Does this require our building Wi‑Fi or PMS software?

No. Sensors run on a dedicated long-range wireless network that operates independently of tenant Wi‑Fi and property-management software, with no IT tickets or software integrations required.

Will walk-in cooler defrost cycles spam my phone?

They should not. Every walk-in sensor is configured with a 15-30 minute defrost-cycle delay designed to filter routine defrost cycles and reduce nuisance alerts. Only sustained temperature rises or propped-open doors send alerts.

Does wireless work inside a stainless-steel walk-in?

Yes, because we never mount the transmitter inside the metal box. The wireless transmitter mounts cleanly outside the walk-in wall, with a thin temperature probe routed through the gasket or port alongside a door-ajar contact.

Who gets alerted at 2 AM, and does this replace my team?

Alerts route directly to the on-site or on-call contacts named in your agreed Alert Response Plan, with clear escalation steps if the first contact is unavailable. NestShield oversees device health and alert routing; we support your team. NestShield does not provide staffed 24/7 emergency dispatch.

Do you cut pipes or open electrical panels?

Point leak, freeze, humidity, float, condensate, and walk-in sensors deploy with zero electrical or plumbing cuts. Whenever a deployment includes panel-mounted runtime clamps (CTs) or inline plumbing/shutoff work, a licensed electrician or licensed plumber handles that scope.

How fast can we launch a 30-day pilot?

A focused pilot for one mechanical room or walk-in cooler deploys quickly with minimal disruption to residents or guests, letting you verify alert timing and team escalation before scaling across buildings.
