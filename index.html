>60% RH

Humidity, condensate & pumps.

Indoor humidity above 60% drives condensation and moisture damage, while clogged condensate pans and failing sump or lift pumps trigger repeat weekend callbacks.

$20K–$40K

Walk-in cooler & freezer loss.

A single overnight compressor failure or propped walk-in door can cost hotel kitchens and foodservice operators $20,000–$40,000 in spoiled inventory and lost revenue.

Silent Flow

Running toilets & hidden leaks.

Point sensors catch drips on the floor, while optional continuous-flow monitoring flags running toilets and silent plumbing leaks before a surprise utility bill arrives.

FAQ

Common questions.

Answers to the questions property managers and asset managers ask before signing.

Will install disrupt tenants or operations?

No. Most sensors are wireless and battery-powered. There is no cutting, drilling, or water shutoff. We schedule by floor during normal business hours. Your facilities team gets a coordination brief before we start.

How many false alarms will we get?

Very few. Equipment and alert rules are matched to each monitored location. Point sensors detect water where it should not be, while flow-monitoring systems can identify abnormal usage patterns and duration. Alerts include the property and exact monitored location.

Does this replace my maintenance team?

No. NestShield runs on top of your existing team. Your staff gets a clean alert with exact location and severity, so reactive emergency calls turn into scheduled dispatches. We onboard your team and give you a written escalation playbook for your SOPs.

Why not just buy sensors off Amazon and do it ourselves?

Sensors alone are detection. They don’t tell you where to put them, when to replace them, or how to package the data for your insurance carrier. NestShield adds the site review, installation planning, staff coordination, continuous automated monitoring, 24/7 alert delivery, documented escalation procedures, and insurance-ready paperwork. You’re paying for accountability, not parts.

What happens when sensors fail or get old?

NestShield manages scheduled battery service, failed-device replacement, and system lifecycle planning under the selected monitoring agreement. Exact coverage, replacement terms, and upgrade provisions are defined in each service proposal.

How long does install take?

Roughly one day per 100 units. 90%+ of sensors need no permits, cutting, or utility shutoffs. The optional shutoff valves require brief scheduled water outages coordinated with your maintenance team.
